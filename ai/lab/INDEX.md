# The ledger — every idea investigated, and how it came out

**Search this before starting anything.** One row per question ever asked, newest first. The verdict
is the point: a **refuted** row with a number attached saves the next person the same afternoon.

| verdict        | means                                                                             |
|----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `confirmed`    | the hypothesis held up under the measurement the entry named                      |
| `refuted`      | it did not — the idea is wrong, or too small to be worth acting on                |
| `inconclusive` | measured, but the measurement could not decide; the row says what is still needed |
| `shipped`      | a change was made, verified, and kept                                             |
| `reverted`     | a change was made and taken back out; the row says why                            |
| `parked`       | blocked on something outside our control; the row says on what                    |

Rows before 2026-08-19 were reconstructed from `journal.md` and the plan files when this ledger was
introduced; their detail lives there rather than in a per-question entry.

## A pattern worth not forgetting

The most consequential fact in this codebase — that the computation is plain Java and only the views
are JavaFX — was established by **measurement**, and the imports said otherwise. `SplitNetworkLayout`,
`ComputeTreeLayout` and `NetworkLayout` all import `javafx.scene.*` and none of them needs a toolkit.
Do not conclude from a grep what a probe would settle.
