# SplitsTree 6.9.9

## Bug fixes

- **K2P distance**: the exact Kimura two-parameter distance applied the corrections for invariable sites and for
  gamma-distributed rates to the wrong quantities, the reciprocals of 1 - 2P - Q and 1 - 2Q rather than to those
  values themselves. With a proportion of invariable sites of zero and no gamma rates, the default, the result
  was correct; with either option set it was not, and the gamma-corrected distance did not agree with the
  formula of Jin and Nei (1990). The corrections are now applied as they are for the Jukes-Cantor and F81
  distances. Maximum-likelihood K2P distances were never affected

- **Add-algorithm menu in the workflow side bar**: the menu of the + button kept the items of the data node that
  was selected first, when the selection moved on to another data node. It now always lists the algorithms for
  the node that is selected
- **World map**: opening a document with a world map view could throw a NullPointerException while the view was
  not yet showing

## Touch screens (iOS app)

- **Box selection**: after a long press and drag, the selection rectangle stayed open and nothing was selected.
  The rectangle now follows the finger also while it passes over edges and labels, the view no longer pans
  under it, and lifting the finger completes the selection
- **Tanglegram**: the selection rectangle can be opened anywhere in the view and may span both trees
- **Pinch to zoom**: in the tree view and the DensiTree view, a pinch with the fingers side by side zooms
  horizontally and a pinch with one finger above the other zooms vertically; a diagonal pinch, and any pinch on a
  radial or circular diagram, zooms both. The DensiTree view used to zoom horizontally only
- **Copy Image**: for the tree, tree pages and DensiTree views the copied image was empty, and for the other
  views it had an unfilled bar along the right and the bottom
- **Export menu**: Print and Export Image, which need a printer dialog and a file dialog, are no longer offered
- **Export data**: the dialog is shown inside the window; while it is open, the rest of the window does not
  react, and a tap next to the dialog closes it

## Changed results

- K2P distances computed with the **Prop Invariable Sites** or **Gamma** option set differ from those reported by
  earlier versions; for example, for transition and transversion proportions of 0.1 each, a proportion of
  invariable sites of 0.2 now gives 0.307 rather than 0.282, and a gamma shape of 1 gives 0.277 rather than 0.200.
  Distances computed with the default options are unchanged
